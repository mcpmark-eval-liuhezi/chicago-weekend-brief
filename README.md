## Chicago Fast Facts (from Wikipedia)

- **Population:** ~2.74 million (2020 census) — 3rd-largest city in the U.S.
- **Nickname:** *The Windy City* (also Chi-Town, Second City, City of Big Shoulders)
- **Founded:** Incorporated as a city in 1837; first permanent settler Jean Baptiste Point du Sable in the 1780s
- **Name origin:** From the Miami–Illinois word *šikaakwa* ("wild onion" or "wild garlic"), via French *Checagou*
- **Metro area:** ~9.4 million residents, with a metro GDP over $919 billion (2024)
- **Motto:** *Urbs in Horto* — "City in a Garden"
- **Why it's windy (sort of):** No native shrub of its own, Chicago sits on the western shore of Lake Michigan, and its dense downtown creates famously gusty streets.
